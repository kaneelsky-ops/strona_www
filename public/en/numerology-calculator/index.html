What does your name sound like in numbers?

It is best to use the name from your birth certificate. Accented letters are converted automatically. All calculations happen only in your browser.

What is the Expression number in numerology?

The Expression number, also called the Name number or Destiny number, is one of the core numbers in numerology. It is calculated from all the letters of your full birth name and symbolises how you express yourself and which talents you are here to develop. This numerology calculator works out three numbers: Expression (all letters), Soul Urge (vowels) and Personality (consonants).

How does the numerology calculator work?

We use the Pythagorean numerology system. Each letter has a value from 1 to 9:

Value Letters
1 A, J, S
2 B, K, T
3 C, L, U
4 D, M, V
5 E, N, W
6 F, O, X
7 G, P, Y
8 H, Q, Z
9 I, R

Vowels (A, E, I, O, U) and consonants (Y counts as a consonant) are added up separately, and each sum is reduced to a single digit from 1 to 9. The result is written e.g. as 4/31: the number 4 comes from the sum 31.

Master numbers and karmic numbers in numerology

Master numbers (green)

The double digits 11, 22, 33, 44, 55, 66, 77, 88 and 99 are considered especially intense in numerology. The calculator marks them in green, and after reduction you receive the base number (e.g. 4/22).

Karmic numbers (red)

The numbers 13, 14, 16, 19 and 26 are called karmic debt numbers. Numerology symbolically links them with lessons and themes that may keep returning. The calculator marks them in red as an invitation to reflect, not as a verdict.

Frequently asked questions about numerology

How do I calculate the numerology number of my name?

+

Assign each letter a value from 1 to 9 according to the Pythagorean chart, add the values together and reduce the sum to a single digit. The calculator does it automatically and shows every step.

Does the calculator handle accented letters?

+

Yes. Accented letters such as á, é, ö, ñ or the Polish ą, ł, ż are counted as their plain Latin forms (a, e, o, n, a, l, z).

What is the difference between the Soul Urge number and the Personality number?

+

The Soul Urge number comes from the vowels and describes your inner desires. The Personality number comes from the consonants (Y counts as a consonant) and describes what others see in you.

Can numerology predict the future?

+

No. It is a symbolic and interpretative practice – a starting point for reflection. It does not replace the advice of a doctor, psychologist or therapist.

Is the numerology calculator free?

+

Yes. You can use it without signing up, as many times as you like.
