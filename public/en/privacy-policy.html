1. Controller and contact

The controller of personal data processed in connection with the website https://www.kaneel.pl/ is Alexandra Orin, who runs KANEEL.

For matters concerning privacy, your data and the exercise of your rights, please write to: kaneelsky@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

When contacting us you may provide:

  • your full name;
  • your email address;
  • information about the services you are interested in;
  • optionally, your date of birth;
  • the content of your message and any information you voluntarily provide in further correspondence.

Providing data is voluntary. The current form requires your name and email address. Choosing services, your date of birth and the message are optional. To reply we need a contact address and a description of your request, which you can also provide later in the correspondence. Your date of birth is not required for general contact.

Please share only the information needed to describe your request. Do not send identity document numbers or unnecessary health information through the form.

When you connect to the website, the hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address, request time, the address of the visited page and browser information. The scope of this logging depends on the configuration of the services used to run and secure the website.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We use the data to:

  • reply and conduct correspondence — under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in handling enquiries;
  • take steps at your request before entering into a contract and to provide the agreed service — under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR;
  • fulfil legal obligations, if they arise in connection with providing the service — under Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR;
  • maintain the security of the website and establish, pursue or defend claims — under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter and is not consent to receive unrelated marketing messages.

4. Contact form and email

Automatic sending via the Gmail API

The form sends your message directly from the website. The data is transmitted over an encrypted connection to the KANEEL server function on Vercel, and then to Google in order to send the message to the KANEEL service address configured on the server. You don’t need to open an email app or sign in to Google.

A confirmation appears only once the message has been accepted by the Gmail API. It does not confirm that the recipient has read it. If an error occurs, the form keeps the data you entered. If the result of the connection is uncertain, the message may have been accepted; please do not resend it immediately. The website code does not retry sending automatically.

You can also write directly to kaneelsky@gmail.com. In that case the rules of your own email program and provider also apply.

The integration uses an email account authorised by the person entitled to operate the website. Visitors do not grant access to their own Google mailbox.

The only access scope used is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send, which is used to send messages. The integration does not use permissions to read the mailbox, view contacts or download correspondence history.

Sending involves processing the sender’s and recipient’s data, the subject and content of the message and the technical API response, e.g. the identifier of the sent message. The address entered in the form is used as the reply-to address to allow further contact.

The integration credentials, including OAuth tokens, are stored only on the server side in the environment configuration and are not available to website visitors.

We use information processed in connection with Google APIs solely to provide the described communication feature and its security. We do not sell this data and do not use it for behavioural advertising or for training artificial intelligence models.

Use of data received via Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The person who authorised the integration can revoke access in the connections settings of their Google account. Revoking access stops further use of the integration. It does not automatically delete messages sent earlier. You can request deletion of data held by KANEEL at kaneelsky@gmail.com.

5. Numerology calculator

The numerology calculator performs name calculations locally in your browser. In this feature the data you enter is not sent to KANEEL or to the Gmail API and is not stored by the website in any database.

The data you enter and the result remain in the memory of the current page. The calculator does not store them in cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage. The “Clear” button removes the entry and the result from the current page. After a refresh the calculator code does not restore the previous result; any restoring of fields by your browser depends on its own settings.

The result is symbolic and informational. It is not used to make decisions about you that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect you.

6. Who data may be shared with

Data may be processed by persons authorised to operate KANEEL and by providers of services needed to run the website and handle correspondence, in particular:

  • Vercel — for hosting, running the form’s server function and infrastructure security;
  • email service providers involved in delivering and storing correspondence;
  • Google — for the Gmail API integration and handling of sent correspondence;
  • entities entitled to receive data under applicable law.

We do not make form data public.

Providers may use infrastructure outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Depending on the service and the transfer basis, adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses may apply.

Information about the rules and safeguards described by the providers is available in:

  • Google’s information on data transfers;
  • the Vercel privacy notice.

Information about the transfer bases and how to obtain a copy of the relevant safeguards can be obtained by contacting the controller.

7. How long we keep data

Enquiries that do not lead to an order are kept for no longer than 12 months from the last message in the matter.

If the correspondence leads to the provision of a service, the data needed to perform it is kept for the duration of the service and then to the extent and for the period required by legal obligations or the need to establish, pursue or defend specific claims — until the relevant limitation period expires.

Data entered only into the local calculator is not stored by KANEEL.

The retention period of technical data depends on the hosting service used and its retention settings. The criteria are handling requests, ensuring security and investigating incidents, and in the event of a specific dispute — the need to secure evidence for the appropriate period. The website code does not create an additional log database or archive of form data.

To protect the form against abuse, the server function keeps an attempt counter in the instance memory, linked to a hash of the connection identifier, for a maximum of 10 minutes. The raw IP address is not stored in this counter. The Google integration credentials are kept for as long as the integration is used. When it is switched off, the person managing the website should revoke access and remove them from the server configuration.

This policy does not trigger automatic deletion of messages from the mailbox. Deleting correspondence requires separate action by the person handling the mailbox or appropriate rules set at the email provider.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

The current website code does not set its own cookies or store data in localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB. It does not use analytics tools, advertising pixels or third-party tracking scripts. The website fonts and scripts are served from the same domain as the website.

The calculator does not need to store the data you enter in cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage. Contact form data is not stored in these mechanisms by the website code either.

Your browser may cache website files in line with content delivery mechanisms. Browser settings let you manage the cache and features such as autofill. This does not mean that KANEEL stores calculator data.

The absence of our own cookies and analytics does not mean that the hosting provider does not process technical data. Vercel may process connection data to deliver and secure the website, as described in sections 2, 6 and 7.

If in the future we introduce technologies that require consent, before activating them we will describe their providers, purposes, the scope of data and duration, and provide an appropriate way to manage consent.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, its rectification, erasure or restriction of processing.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interest. The right to data portability applies in the cases set out in the GDPR.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

To exercise your rights, write to kaneelsky@gmail.com. In case of reasonable doubt we may ask for information needed to confirm your identity.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or with the data protection authority in your country of residence.

10. Security

The public website is served over HTTPS. Form data reaches the server function solely to validate it and send the message via the Gmail API. The application does not create an additional database of enquiries; correspondence remains with the email providers.

Access to correspondence should be limited to authorised persons. Messages and credentials should never be placed in public website files or the repository. Application logs should not contain the full content of forms or secrets.

The Gmail API credentials remain on the server side. The function code logs only error categories and technical statuses, without form content, email addresses or secrets. The form uses validation, request origin checks and abuse-limiting mechanisms. The integration rules are described in section 4.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated following changes to how the website works, the services used or the law. The current version is available on this page together with the date of the last update.
