About me

Let’s get to know each other – who I am and where my knowledge comes from

My name is Alexandra Orin. Although I graduated in two technical fields that taught me precision and structure, I have always been most fascinated by people – the human body and its complex energy.

My spiritual journey began very early. As a teenager I would secretly slip away to a mosque, a synagogue or an Orthodox church to explore universal truths and the rich world of symbols. Astrology and numerology entered my life quite naturally, opening the door to a deeper understanding of human nature. For years I hitchhiked across Europe and parts of Asia, absorbing different cultures and ways of seeing the world. Personally, I am a Sagittarius with Cancer rising and a Projector in Human Design, which allows me to deeply empathise with my clients’ energy.

I completed the School of Numerology at the Numerology Academy, an in-person astrology course in Warsaw and a training in releasing trauma from the body in the Netherlands. In my numerology readings and sessions I combine numerology, astrology, Human Design and elements of Chinese medicine and Ayurveda, turning them into warm, clear guidance. Let’s talk about your path.
